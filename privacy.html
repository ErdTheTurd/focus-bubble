Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Blurzo is a visionOS app published by Edward Dunn.

Blurzo does not collect personal data. The app does not create an account, does not include analytics or advertising, and does not send your information to us or to any third party.

Session history and settings stay on your device. If you delete the app, that on-device data is removed with it.

On a physical Apple Vision Pro, Blurzo may request a world-sensing permission so it can look for a table or desk and leave that surface visible inside the bubble. World sensing is processed on the device. The app does not capture, record, or upload a camera feed or passthrough video.

If you have questions about this policy, contact: erdunn706@gmail.com
