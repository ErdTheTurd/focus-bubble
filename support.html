What does Blurzo do?

When you start a focus timer, it wraps the room around you in a dark, soft virtual bubble and leaves an opening over your desk. Your desk stays visible and everything else fades back. When the timer ends, a short chime plays and the bubble fades away.

Does it blur the cameras or the passthrough feed?

No. The name says blur, but the app does not blur, process, or stream the Vision Pro camera or passthrough feed. It dims and covers the room with a virtual bubble, and leaves an opening over your desk.

What is the difference between Dim and Deep?

Dim is a softer surround that takes the edge off the room. Deep is a darker bubble, for stretches of work when you want less of the room in view.

How long can a session be?

You can pick 15, 25, 50, or 90 minutes, or set a custom length up to 180 minutes. You can pause, resume, or end a session early. A compact timer stays near the desk while you work.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no account. Session history and settings stay on your device. The app works offline, and it does not collect analytics or advertising data.

Can it find my actual desk?

On a physical Vision Pro, Blurzo may ask for world-sensing permission so it can look for a table and keep that surface visible inside the bubble. World sensing is processed on the device. The app does not capture or upload a camera feed. If no table is found, and in the Simulator, it uses a built-in desk zone in front of you.

Where do I get the app?

Blurzo will be available on the App Store for Apple Vision Pro. This site will link to the listing once it is live.
