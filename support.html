What does Blurzo do?

When you start a focus timer, it wraps the room around you in a dark, soft virtual bubble and leaves an opening over your desk. When the timer ends, a short chime plays and the bubble fades away.

Does it blur the cameras or the passthrough feed?

No. The app doesn’t blur the camera feed. It dims the room with a virtual bubble and leaves your desk open.

What is the difference between Dim and Deep?

Dim is a softer surround. Deep is darker, for stretches when you want less of the room in view.

How long can a session be?

15, 25, 50, or 90 minutes, or a custom length up to 180 minutes. You can pause, resume, or end early.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no account. History stays on your device. The app works offline and does not collect analytics or advertising data.

Can it find my actual desk?

On a physical Vision Pro, Blurzo may ask for world-sensing permission so it can look for a table and keep that surface visible. World sensing is processed on the device. If no table is found, and in the Simulator, it uses a built-in desk zone in front of you.
